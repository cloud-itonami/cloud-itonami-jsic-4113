# Business Model: Independent Animation Production Studio

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-jsic-4113`
- JSIC: `4113` アニメーション制作業 (Animation Production)
- Industry: independent animation production studio
- Social impact: creative-livelihoods, cultural-expression, local-jobs

## Customer

- production committees and licensors commissioning episodes/shorts
- streaming platforms and channels with recurring delivery cadence
- studios subcontracting cuts (storyboard/layout/key animation/inbetween)
- brands and agencies commissioning promotional animation

## Offer

- storyboard and layout direction
- key animation and inbetween production (robot-assisted reference capture,
  gated)
- color design, background and composite assembly
- delivery packages and channel/distribution operations

## Revenue

- per-cut / per-episode production fee
- monthly production retainer
- distribution revenue share

## Trust Controls

- no publication without talent consent and rights clearance (voice cast,
  music/BGM licensing, underlying IP)
- reference-capture safety constraints require human sign-off to override
- BGM and asset licensing is verified before assembly (assemble-only policy)
- cut, rights and delivery records are auditable, not editable
